# Passport-Camra

## Online / Offline mode (v2.1)

Settings has an **Online | Offline** switch.

* **Online**: photo is locked (encrypted) and sent through the internet relay. Works on any network.
* **Offline**: photo goes straight to the shop PC over the same WiFi, no internet needed.
  On the shop PC open the QR window of the tool, choose *Offline*, tap *Copy link*, send the link to the phone and tap *Paste QR link* in Settings.
  Use *Test offline connection* to check that the PC answers.
